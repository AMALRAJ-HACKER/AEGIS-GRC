# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

## 1.1 Product Overview

AEGIS GRC is an AI-native Governance, Risk and Compliance platform designed to centralize and automate organizational cybersecurity compliance, risk management, evidence collection, security assessments, vendor management and audit operations.

The platform replaces fragmented spreadsheets, manual documentation, disconnected evidence repositories and repetitive compliance workflows with a centralized intelligence-driven workspace.

AEGIS GRC enables organizations to:

- Understand their compliance posture.
- Implement and monitor security controls.
- Manage multiple regulatory frameworks.
- Collect and validate compliance evidence.
- Identify and mitigate cybersecurity risks.
- Monitor third-party vendors.
- Manage internal and external audits.
- Automate employee security workflows.
- Generate compliance documentation.
- Receive AI-powered compliance recommendations.
- Monitor security and compliance continuously.

## 1.2 Product Vision

To become a unified compliance intelligence platform that makes enterprise-grade governance, risk management and regulatory compliance accessible, understandable and operationally efficient for organizations of every size.

## 1.3 Mission

Reduce the complexity, operational cost and manual effort involved in achieving and maintaining cybersecurity compliance.

## 1.4 Core Value Proposition

**One platform. Multiple frameworks. Continuous evidence. Intelligent risk management. Audit-ready operations.**

# 2. MARKET PROBLEM

Organizations face several challenges when managing cybersecurity compliance.

### Problem 1: Fragmented Compliance Operations

Organizations maintain compliance information across spreadsheets, PDFs, emails and separate management systems.

**AEGIS solution:** Centralized compliance workspace with connected frameworks, controls, evidence and tasks.

### Problem 2: Repetitive Compliance Work

The same security control may satisfy requirements across multiple regulatory frameworks, creating duplicated work.

**AEGIS solution:** Cross-framework control mapping and reusable evidence.

### Problem 3: Manual Evidence Collection

Security teams repeatedly request screenshots, documents, access records and other audit evidence.

**AEGIS solution:** Automated evidence collection through integrations, structured manual uploads and evidence review workflows.

### Problem 4: Poor Risk Visibility

Organizations struggle to understand which risks are critical, who owns them and whether remediation is effective.

**AEGIS solution:** Centralized risk register, risk scoring, treatment tracking and continuous reassessment.

### Problem 5: Expensive Audit Preparation

Audit preparation often requires significant manual coordination between compliance teams, engineers and auditors.

**AEGIS solution:** Audit workspaces, evidence mapping, readiness tracking and corrective action management.

### Problem 6: Limited Regulatory Understanding

Small and medium-sized organizations may not have dedicated regulatory specialists.

**AEGIS solution:** AI-powered regulatory explanations, gap analysis, policy assistance and guided remediation.

### Problem 7: Disconnected Third-Party Risk Management

Organizations lack a centralized view of vendor security posture and associated risks.

**AEGIS solution:** Vendor inventory, security questionnaires, risk assessments and recurring reviews.

## 3.2 AEGIS Competitive Strategy

AEGIS should not attempt to compete solely through the number of dashboard widgets or supported frameworks.

Its differentiation should come from combining the following capabilities.

### A. India-First Regulatory Intelligence

Develop strong support for Indian regulatory requirements alongside international frameworks.

Priority coverage:

- Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
- CERT-In Directions, 2022.
- RBI cybersecurity and IT-related requirements.
- Applicable SEBI cybersecurity requirements.
- Applicable UGC cybersecurity requirements.
- ISO/IEC 27001.
- SOC 2.
- NIST CSF.
- GDPR.
- HIPAA.

Framework applicability must be configurable according to organization type, jurisdiction and regulatory obligations.

### B. Unified Compliance Intelligence

Connect:

- Frameworks.
- Controls.
- Policies.
- Evidence.
- Risks.
- Assets.
- Vendors.
- Tasks.
- Audits.
- Employees.
- Regulatory obligations.

A change in one area should be able to identify related records in other areas.

### C. AI-Native Compliance Operations

AI should be embedded throughout the product rather than limited to a standalone chatbot.

Examples:

- Explain a failed control.
- Identify missing evidence.
- Recommend remediation.
- Generate a policy draft.
- Summarize vendor assessments.
- Identify overlapping framework requirements.
- Prepare audit checklists.
- Explain regulatory changes.
- Create tasks from identified compliance gaps.

### D. Compliance for Organizations of Different Sizes

Support:

- Startups preparing for their first SOC 2 audit.
- SMEs implementing ISO 27001.
- Indian companies addressing regulatory requirements.
- Enterprises managing multiple frameworks.
- Consultants managing several client organizations.

### E. Transparent Compliance Intelligence

Every readiness score, AI recommendation and risk calculation should explain its source, methodology and limitations.

AEGIS should pursue five major differentiators:

1. **India-first compliance:** Deep regulatory coverage for Indian organizations, with global framework support.

The goal is to achieve competitive functional depth over time, not to claim feature superiority before it is demonstrated.

# 18. FINAL PRODUCT DEFINITION

AEGIS GRC is not intended to be merely a framework checklist or a risk register.

It is intended to become an interconnected enterprise compliance intelligence platform.

Its central operating model is:

**Discover → Assess → Implement → Collect → Monitor → Remediate → Validate → Report**

Every major module should contribute to this lifecycle.

The long-term product ambition is to bring together the strongest capabilities of compliance automation, enterprise risk management, security operations, privacy management and AI-assisted regulatory intelligence in one extensible platform.

**AEGIS GRC — Where Compliance Meets Intelligence.**

**Simplifying Compliance. Strengthening Security. Empowering Organizations.**
